Frequently Asked Questions.

Simple answers to what most teams ask before joining Probe.

Is SignalSOS a software platform or a methodology?

SignalSOS is a patent-pending adaptive regulatory intelligence methodology that can be deployed as software, embedded into an existing platform, licensed directly, or white-labeled by a strategic partner.

What does SignalSOS actually do?

SignalSOS turns fragmented customer, operational, conduct, risk, and compliance signals into adaptive regulatory intelligence. It detects emerging harm, maps issues to obligations, routes action to accountable owners, and generates regulator-ready evidence artefacts as work happens.

Who is SignalSOS for?

SignalSOS is designed for regulated firms, platform partners, supervisory authorities, and investors focused on the future of regulatory intelligence, evidence generation, and privacy-preserving oversight.

Does SignalSOS replace existing systems?

No. SignalSOS is designed to sit above and across existing systems as an intelligence and evidence layer. It can work with complaints platforms, VOC tools, contact center data, product telemetry, operational systems, case management tools, and compliance workflows.

Will regulators see our raw data?

No. Firm data remains with the firm. Any supervisory benchmarking is opt-in, aggregated, and consented. The goal is not surveillance — it is better evidence for firms, smarter prioritization for supervisors, and stronger protection for consumers.

What does “adaptive” mean?

Adaptive means the taxonomy, scoring logic, obligation mapping, evidence artefacts, and escalation workflows can be tailored by firm, product, market, jurisdiction, regulator, and risk profile.

Can SignalSOS be licensed or white-labeled?

Yes. The methodology can be licensed, embedded into an existing platform, white-labeled for regulated clients, or deployed through a strategic build partner.

Are you looking for early partners?

Yes. SignalSOS is speaking with design partner firms, platform partners, supervisory authorities, and investors who want to shape adaptive regulatory intelligence infrastructure at an early stage.
